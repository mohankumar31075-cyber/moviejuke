    Template
    Drop your CSV here or browse files — columns like name, keyword, product, stage are detected automatically
    What columns can I include?

    Only name is required. Everything else is optional and matched by common aliases.

    keyword
    The Google search term — also accepts search_term, query, google_search.
    product
    Product or service name. Fuzzy-matched to the catalogue.
    stage
    new, contacted, qualified, quoted, won, lost (or CRM words like "Closed Won").
    source
    google_ads, google_organic, google_shopping, direct, referral…
    created_at
    Any parseable date; drives the trend charts.
    budget / quote_value
    Numbers; ₹ and commas are stripped.

    Regenerate a clean dataset

    This wipes the local database and reseeds 248 enquiries across 40 Google searches. Useful when you have been testing imports.

    Where this data comes from

    Google never exposes who searched. This workspace joins two sources: the Google Ads / Search Console query report (impressions, clicks, position) and your own first-party enquiries (form fills, calls, lead forms), linked by search term, landing page and gclid.
